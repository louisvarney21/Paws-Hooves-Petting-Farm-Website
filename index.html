🐾 Meet the Residents

Our Amazing Animals

Every single one of our animals has been rescued, rehabilitated, and given a forever home here at the farm.

🐐Rescued

Clover the Goat

🐐 Pygmy Goat

Our cheekiest resident! Clover arrived as a tiny orphaned kid and has never quite recovered from the attention. She'll eat your map, your hat, and your heart.

Clover has escaped from her enclosure 47 times and counting!
🐑Rescued

Wooly & Pip

🐑 Herdwick Sheep

Brothers rescued from a flooded farm in Devon, these two are inseparable. Wooly will literally follow you home if you let him — he's absolutely convinced he's a dog.

Wooly once got stuck in a child's buggy. Twice.
🐇Rescued

Hazel & Friends

🐇 Lop-eared Rabbits

Our fluffy trio were discovered living in a hutch far too small. Now they have a whole meadow run to themselves and spend their days binkying with pure joy.

Hazel once ate a whole birthday cake left unattended.
🐓Rescued

Henrietta & the Hens

🐓 Battery-Rescued Hens

30 battery-rescued hens who've never known grass. The day they stepped outside for the first time? Pure magic. They now rule the roost — literally.

Henrietta lays the most perfectly oval eggs in Cornwall.
🦆

Dapper & Ducks

🦆 Khaki Campbells

Found waddling along the A30 in heavy rain, this little flock has since become the farm's unofficial welcoming committee. They queue for morning bread like tiny commuters.

Dapper once photobombed a national press photo.
🐷Rescued

Mr Truffles

🐷 Kune Kune Pig

The undisputed star of Paws & Hooves. Mr Truffles was surrendered when his owner moved abroad and has since become Cornwall's most beloved porcine celebrity.

Mr Truffles has 12,000 Instagram followers!

🚗 Getting Here

By car: 4 miles north of Padstow on the B3276. Free parking for 80 vehicles.

By bus: Western Greyhound Route 56 stops at Trevose Head (10 min walk).

Cycling: The Camel Trail brings you within 2 miles. Bike parking available.

Accessibility: Fully wheelchair accessible paths and facilities throughout.
